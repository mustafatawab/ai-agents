# Meridian Systems — Public Charter

*Document ID: MS-PUB-001 · Clearance: L1 (all employees)*

## Mission

Meridian Systems builds retrieval infrastructure that enterprises can trust
with their most sensitive text. Our premise is simple: authorization belongs
in the data layer, not the application layer.

## What we release publicly

This charter, our quarterlySLA commitments, and powered-by notices are
cleared for every employee, including interns and contractors on day one.

## Speak-up policy

Any employee may raise a security concern directly to the audit committee
without escalation through their reporting line. Retaliation for good-faith
reports is a terminable offense.

## Vacation & leave (summary)

Full-time employees accrue twenty-four days of paid vacation per year and may
carry over up to eight days. The complete policy lives in the internal
handbook.
