# Meridian Systems — Internal Employee Handbook

*Document ID: MS-INT-004 · Clearance: L2 (Regular_Employee and above)*

## Equipment & onboarding

Every joinee receives a standard-issue laptop within three business days of
their start date. Access to #general and #company-announcements is provisioned
automatically; team channels require manager approval.

## Travel & expenses

Book travel through the portal at least fourteen days before departure. Meals
are reimbursed up to $65 per day; client entertainment requires pre-approval
above $200 and an attendee list filed within 48 hours.

## Performance reviews

Reviews run twice a year on April 1 and October 1. Ratings are B, B+, and A.
Ratings above B+ require calibration sign-off from two independent managers.

## Confidentiality

This handbook is internal. Do not forward its contents outside the company
domain, and do not share section 7 (pricing floor guidance) with customers.
