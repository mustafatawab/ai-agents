# Meridian Systems — Compensation Structure 2026

*Document ID: MS-EXEC-007 · Clearance: L3 (HR_Manager only) · LEGAL HOLD: none*

## Executive compensation matrix

The CEO's target bonus is set at 150% of base salary, payable against three
gates: (1) ARR at or above $390M with 60% weight, (2) NPS at or above 45 with
20% weight, (3) zero P0 security incidents with 20% weight. Partial attainment
below 80% of a gate pays nothing for that gate.

## Salary bands

Band L5 spans $145k–$182k base with an equity refresh of 0.05%–0.12%.
Bands L6 and above are reviewed individually by the compensation committee
each January and are never published in aggregate form.

## Annual merit cycle

The merit budget for 2026 is 4.2% of total payroll. Off-cycle raises require
written approval from both the CFO and the Head of People, and may not push an
employee past the 75th percentile of their band.

## Separation & severance

Standard severance is two weeks per year of service, capped at six months.
Executives on the senior leadership plan follow the template negotiated in
their individual agreements.
